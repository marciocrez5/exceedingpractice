National Quality Standard

All 7 Quality Areas Covered

Our review spans every Quality Area of the NQS, ensuring your service has a holistic, confident improvement plan — not just isolated goals.

QA 1

Educational Program and Practice

Programming, planning, documentation, and responsive practice aligned with EYLF v2.0 and the MTOP framework.

QA 2

Children's Health and Safety

Health promotion, safe environments, risk management, and emergency and medical procedure practices.

QA 3

Physical Environment

Indoor and outdoor environments that are inclusive, stimulating, and support children's learning and agency.

QA 4

Staffing Arrangements

Educator qualifications, professional development, mentoring, and leadership capacity-building practices.

QA 5

Relationships with Children

Respectful, responsive interactions that foster belonging, wellbeing, and a strong sense of identity.

QA 6

Collaborative Partnerships

Genuine partnerships with families and the community that inform and enrich the service's practices.

QA 7

Governance and Leadership

Effective leadership, governance, and management practices that support ongoing improvement and compliance.

Exceeding NQS

The Three Exceeding Themes

For a service to be rated Exceeding NQS in a Quality Area, it must demonstrate all three of the following themes. Our self-assessment review helps you evidence each one confidently.

⚙️

Embedded in Operations

Practice is consistent, sustainable, and visible across the whole service — not just in isolated incidents or individual educator behaviours.

🔍

Informed by Critical Reflection

Practice is regularly and genuinely reflected upon by educators and leaders, resulting in meaningful changes to how the service operates.

🤝

Shaped by Meaningful Engagement

Practice is authentically shaped by engagement with families and/or the broader community — their voices genuinely inform decisions.
